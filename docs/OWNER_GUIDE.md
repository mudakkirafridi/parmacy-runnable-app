# Pharmacy Hisab 4.1.0 - Roman Urdu check

Nayi APK ko purani app par Update install karein. Uninstall ya Clear data na karein.

1. Registers mein Salary, Loans, Companies, Cashbook, Expenses, Daily sale, Zakat, Owner, Orders aur Profiles kholein. List ke shuru aur aakhir tak scroll karein. Neeche entry/profile/order button apni jagah nazar aaye.
2. Daily sale, Record expense, Pay salary aur Opening account balance ke neeche wale button par tap karein. Sirf aik action ho to form seedha khule; beech mein khaali Add entry menu na aaye.
3. Companies ke Add entry menu mein Pay company / Ograi aaye. Do ya zyada unpaid bills wali company chunain. Total remaining balance sab bills, payments aur confirmed returns ka net baqaya ho.
4. Ograi mein Allocate to bill na ho. Payment purane bill se shuru ho, phir doosre aur agle bill mein lage. 5 bills hon to bhi poori raqam sahi adjust ho. Zyada bills ki payment safe parts mein journal mein aa sakti hai; total cash aik hi raqam ke mutabiq kam hota hai. Advance / extra ke baghair net baqaya se zyada payment reject honi chahiye.
5. Airplane mode mein Expense, Qarz, Company bill ya payment save karein. Form network ka intezar kiye baghair band ho aur record/cash local report mein aa jaye. App band/dobara kholein: pending record baqi ho. Internet on karein; app khuli ho to automatic retry ho. App dobara kholne par bhi upload khud chale.
6. Phone ka internet request ke darmiyan band ho to aik entry do martaba post na ho. Same ID dobara bhejne se server par amount double nahin hota.
7. Server kisi entry ko reject kare, misal ke taur par doosre device se us din ki sale pehle hi lagi ho, to Review banner mein input mehfooz rahe. Server se confirmed balance dobara load ho; rejected entry ko confirmed amount mein na gina jaye. Retry ya Discard rejected change use karein. Discard ke liye current server records load hona zaroori hai. Network timeout wali unknown commit ko chupke se discard nahin kiya jata.
8. Abbas ke naam ka profile pehle ho to doosra Abbas02 aur teesra Abbas03 bane. Space/capital-letter farq se duplicate naam na bane. Purane duplicate names ke document IDs aur journal entries barqarar rahein; financial history wale profile ko pehla naam milne ki priority ho. Online records milne par legacy duplicate spellings khud save hoti hain.
9. All loan people report mein har profile ki aik summary row aaye. Naam par tap karke us shakhs ki dated transactions kholein. Aik shakhs ki multiple transactions ko multiple customers na samjha jaye.
10. More mein Synced aur Production sign in na hon. Sign out, PIN/fingerprint, owners, recycle bin aur baqi options maujood hon. Sign out ke baad login gate kaam kare.
11. All reports par sirf Share PDF ho. Individual/company/loan reports par Share picture bhi rahe.
12. Home ke bare blue card par Cash balance aur chhote overview card mein Daily net sale ho. Quick entry mein sirf Pay company / Ograi, Order aur Company bill hon. Reports ki lambi list Home se hatti ho; Companies ke All bills baqi hon. Dedicated Reports tab barqarar hai.

SQLite mein pending financial commands aur local balances aik sath save hote hain. App active/resumed hone par automatic sync aur retry hoti hai. Android app bilkul band/force-stop ya phone off ho to pending transaction agle app launch par bheji jati hai. Salary/company bills ki allocation aur closed-day rules server par ab bhi validate hoti hain. Settings aur daily close server confirmation use karte hain.

Physical Samsung keyboard, fingerprint, install/update, reboot aur real WhatsApp sharing phone par check karein. Automated checks physical-device acceptance ka badal nahin.
